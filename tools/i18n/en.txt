h168 | In principle, life insurance is not part of the estate. It therefore escapes the rules protecting forced heirs, such as the reduction of excessive gifts and bequests, as well as the provisions on the collation of gifts, which aim to guarantee equality between heirs.
h169 | However, the Insurance Code provides that manifestly excessive premiums paid into a life insurance contract may be brought back into the estate. The issue is to assess the proportionality between the amounts paid into the contract and the deceased's income or assets.
